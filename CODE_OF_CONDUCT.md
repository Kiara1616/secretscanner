# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in SecretScanner a respectful, inclusive and harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive and considerate.
- Focus feedback on ideas and code, not individuals.
- Protect private information and never publish another person's credentials.
- Accept reasonable moderation decisions.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, threats, deliberate disclosure of private information and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through the contact options on their GitHub profile. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary.

This policy is adapted from the principles of the Contributor Covenant.
